money: privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

money is a personal finance tracker that Aaron Lees wrote for himself. It is not a public service. The only person who can connect accounts to it is Aaron, and the only accounts it reads are his own.

What it reads

Through openfeed, under Australia's Consumer Data Right, money reads the accounts Aaron chooses to share: account names and balances, transactions, and energy accounts, bills and usage. It never sees a bank password.

What it does with it

  • Matches bills to the payments that settled them, and tracks spending and balances.
  • Stores the data on Aaron's own server in Australia. It is not sent to any other service.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or sent to analytics.

Stopping and deleting

Revoking money's access in openfeed stops any new data at once. Data already fetched stays on Aaron's server until he deletes it, and he can delete it at any time.
